## 3. Regulatory environment — 2025–2026

### USP <382> (effective December 1, 2025)
- Replaces USP <381>.
- System-level functional suitability testing of elastomeric
  components (stoppers, plungers, septa, ferrules) within fully
  assembled packaging systems.
- Responsibility shifts from elastomer supplier to drug manufacturer.
- 30-sample MALL acceptance criterion.
- USP explicit: "early adoption is encouraged" — unusual language from
  a pharmacopeial body.

### EU GMP Annex 1
- Validated deterministic CCIT required.
- Visual inspection alone is not acceptable.
- Grade A bioContamination limit = zero.
- Lyophilizers manually loaded without barrier technology require
  sterilization before every batch from August 25, 2024.

### FDA Agentic AI Deployment (December 1, 2025)
- Available to all FDA employees.
- Secure GovCloud environment — does not train on industry submissions.
- Use cases: meeting management, pre-market reviews, review validation,
  post-market surveillance, **inspections**, compliance.
- Voluntary adoption + two-month internal "Agentic AI Challenge"
  showcased at FDA Scientific Computing Day, January 2026.

## 6. Cell-and-gene therapy — technical and market signal

### Manufacturing failure rates (UK National CAR-T Panel, 2025 data)
- 3.87% manufacturing failure in large B-cell lymphoma (LBCL).
- Up to 25% in non-Hodgkin lymphoma.
- Cryopreservation quality identified as a critical determinant of
  overall manufacturing success.

### Cryogenic CCIT technical reality
- Elastomer stopper shrinkage at temperatures below -80°C, and loss
  of elasticity approaching -150°C, can compromise the seal interface.
- Gas exchange into the container headspace creates contamination
  ingress on thaw.
- CAR-T programs require ≤-150°C preservation.

### Container responses (2025–2026)
- SCHOTT TOPPAC freeze — polymer syringe validated to maintain CCI at
  -180°C for cell/gene therapies (May 2025).
- SCHOTT deep-cold PFS — prefilled syringes for -100°C drugs
  (May 2025).
- Stevanato Nexa Flex — COP/COC PFS platform (January 2025).
- CellSeal closed cryo-bag formats.

### BioPlan signal
- 82.6% of cell-and-gene therapy facilities outsource at least some
  manufacturing — the highest outsourcing rate of any modality tracked.
- 87.3% prioritize quality and compliance over cost in CMO selection.

**Implication:** the cell-and-gene segment is structurally
over-outsourcing, structurally quality-prioritizing, and structurally
under-served on CCIT methods that do not yet exist for cryogenic
formats. This is the highest-margin growth vector available to a
specialty CCIT laboratory in 2026.
